# Love Cards

**Love Cards** is a fun and interactive web application that lets users create, customize, and share digital love cards. Perfect for expressing your feelings and making special occasions memorable, this project showcases creativity with a simple and beautiful UI.

---

## Introduction

Love Cards provides a platform to design heartfelt digital cards using a variety of templates, stickers, and customization options. Whether for Valentine's Day, anniversaries, or just to show someone you care, Love Cards makes sharing your emotions easy and visually appealing.

---

## Features

- 🎨 **Card Designer:** Choose templates and personalize your love card with custom messages, colors, and decorations.
- 🖼️ **Image & Sticker Support:** Add images and stickers to enhance your card’s look.
- 💌 **Share & Download:** Download your finished card or share it directly via social media and messaging apps.
- 🕹️ **Responsive UI:** Works great on mobile and desktop devices.
- ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight:** Built for quick load times and smooth interactions.

---

## Tech Stack

- **HTML** (51.1%) – Structure and content of the app.
- **CSS** (30.6%) – Styling, layout, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** (18.3%) – Interactive features and user input handling.

## How to Use

1. **Visit the Website:**  
   Open [`https://ajaymahato431.github.io/love_cards.github.io/`](https://ajaymahato431.github.io/love_cards.github.io/) in your browser.

2. **Create Your Card:**  
   - Select a card template from the gallery.
   - Enter your custom message.
   - Decorate using stickers, colors, and images.

3. **Preview & Share:**  
   - Preview your card.
   - Download the card as an image or share directly using provided options.
